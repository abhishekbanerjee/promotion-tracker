# Promoted Team Survival — Europe's Top Leagues

Tracking the fates of every promoted team across Europe's top leagues over the past ~20 years.

## What we're looking at

Each season, three clubs earn promotion to the top flight. Most struggle. Some survive. A few thrive. This project maps out what actually happens to promoted teams across the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, and Ligue 1 — and asks whether the patterns have changed over time as financial gaps between leagues and clubs have widened.

## Outcome categories

- **Immediately relegated** — back down after one season (yo-yo clubs)
- **Survived, then relegated** — held on for a year or two before going down
- **Established mid-table** — found a stable foothold in the top flight
- **Overperformed** — reached European competition or better

## Questions we want to answer

- Which league is hardest to survive in after promotion?
- Which leagues produce the most chronic yo-yo clubs?
- Has the survival rate changed over time as financial gaps widen?
- Does finishing 1st vs. 2nd vs. playoff winner in the second division predict survival?
